# Spreadsheets: Excel and Google Sheets

Inspect and reproduce:

- workbook and sheet order, names, visibility, tab colors, and sheet direction;
- column widths, row heights, freeze panes, merged cells, print areas, and page setup;
- values, formulas, array formulas, number formats, named ranges, links, and references;
- fills, borders, fonts, alignment, wrapping, indentation, and protection;
- conditional formatting, data validation, filters, tables, pivots, slicers, and charts;
- comments/notes, images, drawings, sparklines, and accessibility metadata.

Preserve formulas and references; never replace functioning cells with displayed values merely to match a screenshot. Keep dates, currencies, percentages, and locale-sensitive formats semantically correct.

For Google Sheets, map unsupported Excel features to the closest editable Sheets behavior and disclose the mapping. Do not claim an uploaded or shared Google Sheet exists unless the connector confirms creation.

Validate representative data states, not only the initial view. Check formulas, merged regions, filters, frozen panes, print layout, charts, and LTR/RTL sheet navigation.
