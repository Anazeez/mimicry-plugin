# Presentations: PowerPoint and Google Slides

Inspect and reproduce:

- slide size, theme colors, theme fonts, masters, layouts, backgrounds, and guides;
- placeholders, text hierarchy, paragraph spacing, bullets, columns, and autofit behavior;
- shape geometry, fills, strokes, shadows, transparency, rotations, and corner radii;
- z-order, grouping, alignment, distribution, clipping, masks, and image crops;
- native tables, charts, diagrams, icons, links, transitions, animations, and speaker notes.

Clone masters and layouts from a native source when possible. For screenshot/PDF sources, create reusable layouts for repeated frames rather than duplicating loose objects.

Keep text editable. Keep charts data-backed when the source exposes data; otherwise rebuild the visible chart with editable series and disclose inferred values. Preserve source reading order and alt text.

Validate by rendering every slide at the exact source aspect ratio. Check text reflow, crops, z-order, alignment, repeated chrome, and RTL reading order.
